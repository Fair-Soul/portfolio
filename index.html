    01 · The problemBefore AezakmiSO

    Before: manual checks or paid services

    We run a large portfolio of apps in many countries. Whether an app ranks for its keywords and how long it stays in the store decides if the work pays off.

    Way 01

    Manual checks

    People searched the stores by hand, key by key, country by country.

    × Hours a week, no history
    Way 02

    Paid ASO services

    They cost money and, for our market, showed weaker data than what we saw in the stores ourselves.

    × Paid, and less accurate

    Everyday jobBeforeIn AezakmiSO

      01 · Where the first version stoppedAudit of my first version

      Problem: the first version showed numbers but didn’t explain them

      It had polished screens, but did not answer the main question: what is my body telling me, and what do I do? I went through every screen and listed what was missing.

      01No first runOnboarding and a 7-day baseline. No conclusions before there is data.
      02Numbers without meaningEvery insight names the data behind it and how sure it is.
      03An AI that could say anythingHard rules: no diagnoses, a source on every answer, urgent cases go to a person.
      04No way back from an insightOne action to try, then a check-in to see if it helped.
      Wrong rangeBlood oxygen 88% was labelled “healthy”. Fixed to 97%.
      Impossible metricA wrist band can’t measure glucose (FDA warning, 2024). Removed.
      Numbers disagreed10k steps next to 20 km walked. Fixed to 7.6 km.
      Repeated insightThe same sleep tip sat in four categories. Now it lives in one place.
      02 · Product logicSignal · insight · action · check-in

      The core loop: signal, insight, action, check-in

      01Signal 02Insight 03Action 04Check-in
      1. 01SignalBand data plus a 10-second check-in about how you feel.
      2. 02InsightA pattern, the evidence behind it and how sure the app is.
      3. 03ActionOne small thing to try for a week. Not a list of tips.
      4. 04Check-inDid it help? The answer feeds the next signal.
      5. !!Urgent branchIf a signal is dangerous, the loop stops and the app points to a person.
      5 headache days in 14. 4 came after a night under 6 hours.

      The example that runs through every screen · demo data

      01 · ContextWhat Kolesa already knows

      What Kolesa’s own research says about trust

      In its own 2025 research (35,000 surveys and 500 meetings) Kolesa found that buying a car starts with checking the history and reading reviews. It also fights fraud at scale:

      1M+daily app users · 5.8M monthly
      250Mmessages in the Kolesa chat in 2025
      65,000listings checked a day, at most
      72,000fake photos blocked
      25,000dishonest listings blocked
      1Mspam or suspicious messages blocked
      02 · Fraud pathThe typical scheme

      How a typical scam goes: four steps

      01A price far below marketMy solution → Listing · three questions
      02“Let’s move to WhatsApp”My solution → Chat · warning at the moment
      03Photos of documentsMy solution → Documents · inside the app
      04Prepayment “for booking”My solution → Safe deal · money held

      In May 2025 prosecutors and the platforms blocked 5,000+ accounts that worked this way.

      03 · AuditLive app, 16 Nov 2026

      I went through the app as a buyer. Here’s what I found

      What already works:Quick replies in the chatA history requestAI stop-word warningsModeration at scale
      04 · IdeaOne flow instead of scattered tools

      Idea: make the chat a guided deal

      Today the chat is where Kolesa loses the buyer to WhatsApp and the phone. In my version it becomes a deal with five steps, and each step brings the right check at the right moment instead of one banner for everything.

      The loop:A deal in Kolesa→A review only after it→Seller reputation→Higher in search→More deals in Kolesa
      05 · Inside the dealOne buyer, one car, one seller

      The deal step by step, from first message to keys

      01 / 09

      1. 01
        Listing

        Three questions under the price: is it the same car, is the seller real, is the price fair. A missing history is a visible open item.

      2. 02
        История авто

        The requested history arrives in the deal, with a “what next” list.

      3. 03
        Chat

        Step 1 of 5. The WhatsApp warning appears when the seller suggests it. Quick replies become actions.

      4. 04
        Call

        Through Kolesa: numbers hidden, the call is recorded in the chat.

      5. 05
        Inspection

        Day, time, place. A mechanic checks the accident spot from the history. No booking prepayment.

      6. 06
        On-site check

        The VIN on the body is matched against the history; the checklist is built from it.

      7. 07
        Documents

        Checked in the app, never as photos in messengers. The contract fills itself from the listing.

      8. 08
        Payment

        Kaspi installment first, the way most people buy. Money is held until re-registration.

      9. 09
        Review

        Only after a real deal. It feeds the seller profile and their place in search.

      06 · When it failsEdge case

      When a check fails, the deal pauses

      →

      If the odometer doesn’t match the history, payment stays locked. The buyer can ask the seller in the chat, report the listing with the evidence attached, or walk away without losing anything.

      How I’d know it works.

      Share of first contacts that stay in the chatToday the app itself pushes buyers to an untracked call
      Share of deals that reach an inspection booked in KolesaShows the deal flow is used, not skipped
      Prepayment fraud reports per chatThe core safety outcome
      Share of sellers with reviews from real dealsFeeds the trust loop and search ranking
